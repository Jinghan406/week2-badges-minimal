In use

Tooltip · one drop, many anchors

Badge · no content

None → a bud swells on the icon's shoulder, necks and pinches off → a 12px dot, 3px clear of the icon. Click again and it rejoins and sinks back in.

Badge · with content

Same bud, then it widens into a pill and the count or word fades in once the edge is crisp. Absorbing plays the same path backwards, content first.

Tooltip · plain

Hover: a drop leaves the top of the anchor, rises 8px clear and flattens into a one-line label. Leave: it falls back and merges.

Tooltip · rich

Same drip, grown into a card with a title, a line of context and one action. It stays open while the pointer crosses into it, so the action can be reached.
